## Mathematical status

The principal result is resolved within the stated model:

\[
\operatorname{sign.dim}(\diamond_6)=3.
\]

The upper bound is an exact computer-assisted proof over every primitive
measurement. It is not a sampled computation or a floating-point inference.
The lower bound is imported from Bencze--Frenkel. The boundary consequence
`sign.dim(diamond_d)=3` exactly for positive integers `3 <= d <= 6` combines
the new dimension-six case with prior results.

The result is unrefereed and has not been checked in a proof assistant. The
ordinary correctness of the cited mathematics, the interpreter/compiler, and
the inspected encodings remains in the trust boundary.
