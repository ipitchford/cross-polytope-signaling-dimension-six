# Component licence map

The package deliberately separates creator-controlled research content from
software and third-party material.

| Component | Licence/status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `paper.tex`, generated `paper.pdf`, accessible `paper.md`, README, status, assurance, provenance, AI index, structured claims, source map, citation audit, review response, research-gate record, environment and replay records | CC0-1.0 | Original creator-controlled prose and public release records |
| `data/manifest.json`, generated graph-class data, catalogues and other original generated research data | CC0-1.0 | Facts may not be copyrightable; CC0 applies to any rights that do exist |
| Original files under `code/` and `checks/` | MIT | See `LICENSE-CODE` |
| `bibliography.bib` | CC0-1.0 for original compilation and annotations; underlying bibliographic facts are not claimed | Citations do not license cited works |
| Cited papers and books | Upstream terms / `NOASSERTION` | Not bundled and not relicensed |
| Supplied private review prose and raw review package | Not distributed; upstream rights retained | Findings are summarized, not copied wholesale |
| Raw search-result payloads and external minimal-balanced catalogue | Not distributed; upstream rights retained | The external catalogue was not obtained or compared |
| nauty and `genbg` | Upstream nauty licence | External dependency; not licensed under this repository's MIT notice |
| Python, C++ compiler, operating system, actions and other toolchains | Upstream terms | External dependencies |
| Fonts embedded in a generated PDF | Upstream font terms | Embedding does not transfer font ownership or relicense font software |

The original private source archive did not arrive with a CC0 dedication. The
public-domain dedication is applied at publication to original content the
scholarly creator is entitled to dedicate. No inclusion, citation, link, hash,
or description expands that scope.

When redistributing a mixed subset, retain the applicable notices and do not
represent a third-party component as CC0 or MIT unless its own licence permits
that statement.
